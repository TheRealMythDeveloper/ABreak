# A Break 

**A quiet little game about slowing down.**

*A Break* is a small browser-based game built around a simple idea: sometimes you need to step away from everything, take a breath, and just exist for a little while.

Explore a peaceful forest, move at your own pace, and listen to a message that unfolds as you travel. There is no timer chasing you, no score to beat, and no demand to hurry.

## About

Life can get overwhelming. Between responsibilities, expectations, distractions, and the constant feeling that time is running out, it can be difficult to stop for a moment.

*A Break* was created as a small space away from that.

As you travel through the forest, dialogue gradually unfolds. The world contains small obstacles, gaps, springboards, and platforms, but it isn't meant to be a stressful challenge. It's a place to explore.

## What It has to Offer

- *a world*
  A long, peaceful walk.
- *platforming elements*
  for you to enjoy as you listen and watch.
- *ambient music*
  Optional music to accompany your journey.

## How to Play

1. Clone or download the repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Launch `index.html` in a browser.
4. Start exploring.

Or, go to the [Game Page](https://therealmythdeveloper.github.io/ABreak/)

No installation or additional dependencies are required.

## The Purpose

This project is an experiment in creating something that isn't about competition, efficiency, or productivity.

It's about creating a moment of quiet.

A reminder that it's okay to stop, take a break, and keep going when you're ready.
It's also a place for me to show what it's like if I code without any AI at all. 
It's a place I made so I don't feel I have to prove myself to all.
